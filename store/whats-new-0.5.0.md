# What's new in 0.5.0 (Play "Release notes", max 500 characters per language)

The same alarms, settings and history carry over from 0.4.1: it is an update of the same app.

## en-US

<en-US>
A new, faster app: every screen is drawn natively, so it opens quicker, scrolls smoothly and uses less memory. Your alarms, settings and history stay as they were. Also: Back works on Android 14 and later, and a touch on a ringing alarm now counts as activity for its ring limit.
</en-US>

## nl-NL

<nl-NL>

Ook: Terug werkt op Android 14 en nieuwer, en een aanraking van een afgaande wekker telt nu mee voor de beltijdlimiet.
</nl-NL>
