# What's new in 0.5.2 (Play "Release notes", max 500 characters per language)

## en-US

<en-US>
Works with TalkBack: every button, switch and alarm card now has a clear name and role, sheets and dialogs keep the screen reader inside them, messages like "Alarm deleted, Undo" are announced, and lists keep your place when they refresh. Also: the alarm label is trimmed, and a word being typed no longer overlaps the placeholder on some keyboards.
</en-US>

## nl-NL

<nl-NL>

Ook: het wekkerlabel wordt opgeschoond en een woord dat je typt overlapt de plaatsaanduiding niet meer bij sommige toetsenborden.

## es-ES

Además: la etiqueta de la alarma se recorta y una palabra que escribes ya no se solapa con el texto de ayuda en algunos teclados.
